[ANNOUNCEMENT] Launch of the New Student Portal for Summer 2026
Course Registration
June 29, 2026
Dear VinUni Students,
We are pleased to announce that starting from the Summer 2026 semester, all course registrations will officially be conducted
through the new VinUniDigi Student Portal.
The new portal offers a more intuitive interface and an improved registration experience, allowing students to search, register,
review, and manage courses more efficiently.
Please read the following guidelines carefully before the registration period begins.
 Course Registration Handbook
To help students become familiar with the new registration system, the Office of the Registrar has prepared a Course Registration
Handbook with detailed instructions and screenshots.
Dow nload: Course Registration_Handbook
The handbook includes guidance on:
Signing in to the Student Portal
Registering for courses
Understanding class status and common registration issues
Reviewing registered classes
Dropping registered classes
Previewing your timetable
Final registration checklist
We strongly recommend reviewing the handbook before your registration slot opens.
 Access the Student Portal
Student Portal:https://one.vinuni.edu.vn/student
Sign in using your assigned VinUniversity email account and password.
 Important Registration Rules
Class Status
Please pay attention to the class status before registering.
Available – Registration is allowed.
Full – No seats are available.
Conflict – The class overlaps with another registered class.
Prerequisite Requirem ents
The system automatically checks prerequisite and pre-study requirements. Registration will not be permitted if these requirements
have not been satisfied.
Dropping a Course
Students may drop a registered class only while the registration period is open.
To drop a class:
Click the Delete icon next to the course.
Select OK to confirm.
 Final Registration Checklist
Before completing your registration, please ensure that you have:
Signed in with your VinUni account.
Selected the correct academic term.
