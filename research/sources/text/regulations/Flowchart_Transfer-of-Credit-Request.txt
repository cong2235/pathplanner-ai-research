- Assessment of an application for credit transfer takes into 
consideration the followings:  
(i) type of studies undertaken 
(ii) institution where the studies were undertaken 
(iii) amount of study 
(iv) learning outcomes 
(v) University’s/College’s limits on credit transfer 
(vi) any restrictions placed by relevant external 
professional accreditation or registration bodies on the 
amount and type of credit.

- Depending on the assessment, the decision may be:  
Option 1: Decline (students need to enroll in the course)

the final exam with a passing score, taking the practical 
sessions, etc). The relevant year Committee or the Program

6 Registrar   - An official transcript (bearing the institutional seal) from

the University Registrar Office for final approval and

application will be required for further approvals.
