Dear VinUniversity Student, 
 
I am truly excited to welcome you to the VinUniversity 
family. You have persisted through all the challenges to 
find yourself here where you dreamed to be. You deserve 
congratulations and so do the wonderful people who have

VinUniversity. This Guide covers important topics such as academic requirements,
first-year experience, residential life, student activities, and many more. This Guide
will help you to be aware of and understand essential policies, values, and standards
that you should follow as a valued community member. Here you find the
community's expectations of you in terms of honesty, personal conduct, and
commitment to a safe living and learning environment. This Guide is available
online and will be updated regularly with input from the VinUniversity community.
Therefore., I urge you to take a few minutes to read through this document and feel
free to research and dig deeper into those areas of the university that spark your
interest.

competent and skilled college graduate, but also as an informed, empathetic,

VinUniversity, you will grow and develop your talents to become the future leader

witnessing you grow and thrive through your years at VinUni.

4.1. Generic/Academic Advising
Generic/Academic advising is a student-centered process that allows you to reach
your full academic potential and develop professional competency under the
guidance of an advisor. It is a shared responsibility between you and your advisor to
create a plan for your academic journey. However, you are ultimately responsible to
define your academic and career goals and execute your plan to successfully achieve
them. 
Your Generic/Academic Advisor will support you throughout your university life and is
assigned based on your major. During your first year at VinUniversity, your generic
advisor will support your transition from high school to university life with your
decisions to choose your classes and student organizations to join and navigate the
policies and guidelines of VinUniversity.   From your second year onwards, Academic
Advisor will continue to help you build on the academic, co-curricular, and career
goals throughout your university life. 
All VinUniversity students are required to meet with their   generic advisor at least
once per semester in their first academic year. Your first appointment should take
place before the end of the add/drop period. We will advise you on how to make an
appointment with your advisor. If the advisor permits it, you may also contact your
advisor directly through his/her work email.

4.3. Learning Management System
VinUniversity uses Canvas, a Learning Management System (LMS), which is a web-
based software application that is used for all the VinUni for credit courses and many
non-credit, informal learning opportunities as well. Canvas is used by instructors to
maintain course content, schedule course events, communicate with students in the
course, assign activities, assignments and quizzes, assess participation and maintain
the course grades. Students are expected to login to Canvas each day to keep track of
their course activities and requirements.

Academic requirements to earn a bachelor’s degree
Every undergraduate student needs to fulfill the following academic requirements:
Complete a minimum credit requirement for the program of study.
Complete the requirements of the General Education and English proficiency.
Complete the requirements for your major, defined by each College.

academic plan on the Student Portal: https://sis.vinuni.edu.vn/ which reflects your

common for every undergraduate.

8.4. Academic Integrity
Absolute integrity is expected of every VinUniversity student in all academic
undertakings. Integrity entails a firm adherence to a set of values, and the values most
essential to an academic community are grounded on the concept of honesty with
respect to the intellectual efforts of oneself and others. Academic integrity is expected
not only in formal coursework situations but in all University relationships and
interactions connected to the educational process, including the use of University
resources. 
A VinUniversity student’s submission of work for academic credit indicates that the
work is the student’s own. All outside assistance should be acknowledged, and the
student’s academic position truthfully reported at all times. In addition, VinUniversity
students have a right to expect academic integrity from each of their peers.
A. General Responsibilities
1.  A student shall in no way misrepresent his or her work.
2. A student shall in no way fraudulently or unfairly advance his or her academic
position.
3. A student shall refuse to be a party to another student’s failure to maintain
academic integrity.
4. A student shall not in any other manner violate the principle of academic integrity.

11.2. Integrated Degree Programs
(Based on agreements between VinUni and a partner university)
Students who are accepted into this program will have the opportunity to earn two
bachelor’s degrees or a bachelor’s and a master’s degree – one from VinUni and the
second degree from a partner university abroad.
The program consists of two phases in which VinUni students will study Phase 1 at
VinUni and Phase 2 at a partner university overseas. At the end of both phases, they
will get two degrees, one from VinUni and another from the partner university. The
modules are often in the form of 3+2, 4+1, 3.5+2 for Bachelor’s – Master’s programs
and 2+2 or 2.5 +1.5 for Bachelor’s – Bachelor’s programs.
Fast-tracking students take classes towards their Master’s while earning their
Bachelor’s degree, which helps them save significant costs of attendance and
enable them to graduate up to 12 months sooner. In addition, accelerated degree
pathways are intended to build on the momentum students currently have as an
undergraduate in an effort to create a seamless progression toward higher degree
attainment, in the most efficient and cost-effective manner possible.

18. Work-Study Program
VinUniversity promotes a co-living, co-working, and  co-learning environment as we
do not separate education from life. Work-Study Pro gram not only contributes to
your connections with academics and staff but serves the purposes of fostering your
holistic development and growing as a person. The P rogram is expected to narrow
down the school-to-work gap for you. This paid job aims to deepen your knowledge,
strengthen your skills, and gain career-related exp eriences during your
undergraduate journey at different academic and fun ctional departments at
VinUniversity.  
Work-study students have opportunities to serve as research assistants or teaching
assistants for faculty, and work on the campus in a dministrative and support jobs
such as Library support, lab support, IT desk support, administrative support, etc.

During summer and other seasonal breaks:
